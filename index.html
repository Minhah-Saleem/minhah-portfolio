The Lore

This is not a straight timeline. Straight timelines rarely survive real life.

🌱

Origin Arc

Born on 15 January 1999 in Pakistan. Youngest of three girls. Raised in a remote defense colony near Jauharabad, where the outside world felt far away, but friendships were very real.

Internet? Questionable. Friends? Elite. The lore started early.

🎓

EME / NUST Arc

After FSC, she joined EME, NUST. Her review was approximately: “I do not think I like this place.” She stayed anyway, studied engineering, and collected lifelong friends.

Did she love the campus experience? No. Did she find excellent people? Absolutely.

🕯

The Hard Arc

During university, her mother battled cancer. Her family changed, grief arrived, and life kept moving even when it felt impossible.

This section is quiet on purpose. It is here because it explains the shape of her resilience, not because pain needs decoration.

Some people build ambition from comfort. Some build it while carrying grief carefully.

🦠

COVID + PIEAS Arc

Fourth year became the COVID year. Everyone went home. She started PIEAS, left after a few months, and never returned after the pandemic disruption.

Not every chapter ends with a certificate. Some chapters redirect the plot.

💼

Lahore Episode

First full-time job. Toxic workplace. Exit.

Character development includes knowing when to leave the building.

🏠

Healing Arc

Joined HealthHub. Worked from home. Healed a little. Started believing in herself again. Applied for graduate school. Rejected once. Applied again the next month. Accepted.

Rejection: temporary. Comeback: already loading.

🇰🇷

Korea Arc

Moved to South Korea for a Master’s in Artificial Intelligence at Dongguk University. Then came the research assistantship, publication, patent, industrial collaborations, volunteering, Korean classes, and a lot of “how did we get here?” energy.

Came for the Master’s. Stayed for the lore.
